Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Zizz Interactive ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes mobile games for Android and iOS devices. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our games, websites, and related services ("Services").

By downloading or using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Customer support requests
  • Email address when contacting support
  • Feedback and bug reports
  • Information submitted voluntarily

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system version
  • Language and region
  • Country and approximate location
  • IP address
  • Advertising Identifier (AAID / IDFA where applicable)
  • Crash logs and diagnostics
  • Game progress and gameplay statistics
  • Session duration
  • Purchase history
  • Ad interaction information
  • Fraud prevention signals

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Operate and maintain our games
  • Save game progress
  • Improve gameplay experience
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Analyze player behavior
  • Provide customer support
  • Process in-app purchases
  • Display personalized or contextual advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Advertising

Our games may display advertisements. Advertising partners may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location, advertising identifiers, and usage information to provide relevant advertisements and measure campaign performance. Depending on your region, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized according to applicable privacy laws.

4. In-App Purchases

Some games may offer optional in-app purchases for virtual items, removal of advertisements, premium content, or game currency. Purchases are processed by the relevant platform provider, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

5. Third-Party Services

Our Services may integrate third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, cloud services, crash reporting, attribution, and payment processing. Depending on the game, these may include Google Play Services, Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google UMP, AppLovin, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, ironSource, Adjust, AppsFlyer, Tenjin, and Apple App Store Services. Each provider maintains its own privacy practices, which you should review.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers when necessary to display advertisements, measure ad performance, provide analytics, detect fraud, process purchases, deliver cloud services, improve performance, or comply with applicable laws.

7. Children's Privacy

Some games may be designed for general audiences. Where required, we comply with applicable child privacy laws and platform policies, including Google Play Families Policy, COPPA, and GDPR-K. When a game is directed toward children, we limit data collection and use age-appropriate advertising and required consent processes where applicable.

8. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method is completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary to provide our Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed on servers outside your country. We take appropriate safeguards in accordance with applicable laws.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your information; object to certain processing; withdraw consent; and request data portability.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and certain services may use cookies, SDK technologies, and similar identifiers to improve functionality, analyze traffic, and personalize user experiences.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
